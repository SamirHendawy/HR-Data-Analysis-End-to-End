🎯 Project Overview: Business Problem & Objective
The Problem Statement (Why this project exists):
Despite two decades of continuous organizational growth, the company's executive leadership and HR department have been making talent management decisions based on intuition rather than data. The company has accumulated thousands of employee records, yet lacks clear visibility into critical workforce metrics. They are facing three hidden business risks:
1.	Unidentified Attrition: An inability to pinpoint which specific departments or locations are suffering from high turnover rates (brain drain).
2.	Inefficient Recruitment: A lack of understanding regarding historical hiring seasonality, leading to misallocated recruitment budgets and overwhelmed onboarding teams.
3.	Workforce Fragmentation: Blind spots regarding the demographic makeup and the performance/retention differences between the rapidly growing Remote workforce and the centralized Headquarters.
The Objective (What this project achieves):
The goal of this project is to build an end-to-end Data Analytics Pipeline (SQL $\rightarrow$ Python $\rightarrow$ Power BI) to transition the company from "reactive" HR administration to "proactive", data-driven talent strategy.
By extracting, cleaning, and analyzing over 22,000 employee records, this project aims to:
•	Uncover retention bottlenecks and highlight high-risk departments (e.g., Auditing).
•	Map the exact geographic and demographic distribution of the workforce.
•	Identify actionable hiring seasonality trends to optimize annual recruitment budgets.
•	Deliver an interactive executive dashboard that empowers leadership to monitor workforce health and make strategic decisions instantly.
